**Warm-up (A1 only):** 60% × 5 → 75% × 3 → 90% × 1. Rest 2 min between, 3 min before work set.

**Elbow note:** no skull crushers, no DB overhead extensions. Cable-only triceps work — the cable's line of pull avoids the position that clicks. If loaded overhead work is ever wanted back, EZ bar only, never straight bar.

## 3.7 Whoop Autoregulation

| Recovery | Action |
|---|---|
| Green | As written. Push the top set. |
| Yellow | Skip money set. Start at set 2 load. All volume work retained. |
| Red | Drop RPT compounds. Exercises 3–6 only, 2 RIR. |
| 2× red consecutive | Trigger deload early. |

Adjust intensity, never attendance. On 3 sessions/week a skipped day costs 33% of the week.

## 3.8 Placement Block Contingency

Trigger: any block of 12-hour shifts.

- Reduce to **2 sessions/week** — UPPER-A and UPPER-B only
- Drop LEGS entirely
- First 4 exercises of each session only
- Goal: **hold loads**, do not add
- Resume full programme within 1 week of block ending

**Protein powder note:** 50 g of powder ≈ 40 g of protein. Pea isolate runs 80–85% protein by weight, rice 75–80%. **Verify against the tub label** — products vary by 10 percentage points.

**Ratio progression:** start 30 pea / 20 rice. Hold 2 weeks. If asymptomatic, may shift toward 35/15. Retain rice in the blend permanently for lysine, not for FODMAP reasons — pea is low in methionine and rice covers it.

**Functional components:** kiwi (actinidin — aids protein digestion), banana (resistant starch — motility), Sunfiber (soluble, non-gas-producing). Protein addition slows gastric emptying, blunting the fruit sugar spike. Fibre at ~14 g is acceptable standalone; it is only problematic stacked onto a meal.

**Chia is NOT in this shake.** Two gel-formers in one sitting slows motility. Chia sits at Meal 2.

# 6. DIGESTIVE PROTOCOL

## 6.1 Position

Low-FODMAP is a diagnostic tool with a defined endpoint, not a permanent diet. Long-term strict adherence reduces microbial diversity. It also rules out legumes, wheat and dairy — three of the largest cheap protein and calorie sources, which makes the 2,900 kcal surplus phases materially harder.

DAO supplementation is **symptom management, not repair** — it acts on histamine in the gut lumen for that meal and does not restore endogenous DAO production. Stability on DAO is not evidence of resolution. Sleep and recovery are doing the underlying work.

**Therefore:** test early and casually rather than carrying the restriction past its usefulness. The failure mode is remaining compliant for 12 months after it stopped being necessary and never finding out.

## 6.2 Reintroduction Window
**Phase 2 · Mon 4 Jan – Sun 28 Mar 2027**

Maintenance calories, lowest-stakes phase, feeds into Phase 3.

## 6.3 Order — REVISED

Lactose demoted from first to last. The acne response to dairy is mediated by whey protein and IGF-1 signalling, not lactose — so a lactose challenge confounds two variables and the payoff is low if whey remains excluded regardless.

| Order | Subgroup | Test food | Day 1 | Day 2 | Day 3 | Window |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GOS | Chickpeas, tinned + rinsed | 30 g | 60 g | 100 g | 4–9 Jan |
| 2 | Fructans (wheat) | White bread | ½ slice | 1 slice | 2 slices | 18–23 Jan |
| 3 | Mannitol | Sweet potato | 100 g | 150 g | 200 g | 1–6 Feb |
| 4 | Sorbitol | Blackberries | 3 | 5 | 10 | 15–20 Feb |
| 5 | Fructose | Honey | ½ tsp | 1 tsp | 2 tsp | 1–6 Mar |
| 6 | Fructans (onion/garlic) | Onion | ¼ small | ½ | 1 | 15–20 Mar |
| — | Lactose | *Deferred — whey excluded regardless* | | | | — |

**Protocol:** 3 days challenge, escalating dose, nothing else new. 3 days washout at baseline regardless of outcome — symptoms lag.

**Log per challenge:** symptom score 0–10 · bowel movement quality · bloating · Whoop recovery delta · sleep delta.

Nothing by day 3 = tolerated at that dose. Symptoms = record the dose it appeared at, wash out, retest in 3 months. Do not write a food off permanently.

## 6.4 Histamine — Separate Track

No validated reintroduction protocol exists. Approach is slower and looser: single foods, one at a time, several days apart. **Egg white is the first worth retesting** — highest protein quality available, dairy-free, and currently excluded only as a histamine liberator.

Retain freeze-raw/cook-fresh permanently regardless of outcomes. It costs nothing.

## 6.5 Endpoint

The most liberal diet that keeps symptoms controlled — not the strictest one that does. Retain Sunfiber throughout.

**Clinical note:** FODMAP reintroduction is standard dietetic practice. With a low-histamine framework layered on top, the two protocols interact and misattribution is easy. A GP referral to a dietitian with GI experience would likely achieve more in 6 weeks than 6 months of self-testing.

# 7. SUPPLEMENTS — CHANGES ONLY

| Item | Previous | New | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creatine monohydrate | 10 g | **5 g** | Muscle saturation complete at 3–5 g. Remainder excreted. |
| Sunfiber | 7 g | **12 g** — 7 g Meal 1, 5 g Meal 3 | Bridges to 25–30 g daily fibre without gas-producing volume |
| Protein powder | Beef isolate / vegan | **Pea + rice blend** | Beef isolate category is largely hydrolysed collagen — near-zero DIAAS, poor leucine. Hydrolysis also raises biogenic amine content. |

**Creatine caveat:** brain creatine uptake is slower and less complete than muscle; higher-dose cognition research exists but is early and unsettled. Reverting to 10 g during placement blocks is defensible as a maybe, not a recommendation. Monohydrate only — HCl offers no advantage.

**Unchanged:** NAC · bacopa · ashwagandha KSM-66 · phosphatidylserine · quercetin complex · DAO enzyme · magnesium glycinate · ALCAR · collagen · ProBiota HistaminX. Timing per existing protocol.

**Not required:** Kino Mojo, Nitro, Gains, Octane. Creatine is the only evidence-backed ingredient in that range and is already covered.

## 7.1 Protein Source Ranking — Reference

| Rank | Source | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pea/rice blend | Complementary aminos, no hydrolysis, best overall |
| 2 | Rice alone | Cleanest on all constraints. Arsenic accumulation is the only concern |
| 3 | Pea alone | Higher leucine. GOS ceiling is the only limiter |
| 4 | Potato isolate | Excellent, poor UK availability |
| 5 | Egg white | Best quality, but histamine liberator |
| 6 | Soy isolate | Hormone objection unfounded. Histamine listing is the blocker |
| 7 | Beef isolate | Worst option. Collagen-dominant, hydrolysed |

*Whey and casein excluded — acne via IGF-1 pathway.*

**Arsenic mitigation:** rice protein concentrates soil arsenic. Given existing high white rice intake, rotate sources — 3 months on rice-containing blend, 3 months pea-dominant — or buy a brand publishing third-party heavy metal testing.

**Soy reference:** isoflavones bind ERβ preferentially as weak partial agonists at affinity orders of magnitude below estradiol. Pooled human data shows no significant effect on testosterone or estradiol in men. Soy **lecithin** in whey products is an emulsifier, not soy protein, at a fraction of a percent by weight — hormonally irrelevant.
