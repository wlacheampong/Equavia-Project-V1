09

The monthly read

›

Flexed arm, chest, four photos. Same morning, not after training. Then read it against your lifts.

Arm up · lifts upBlock is working. Don't change anything.
Lifts up · arm flatStrength without size. Check you're eating the surplus.
Arm flat 2 monthsRotate the curl variation. Stimulus has gone stale.
Photos changed · numbers flatMeasurement noise. Trust the photos.
Chest up faster than armVolume drifting to strengths. Recheck the arm sets.
Nothing moved anywhereCheck sessions logged before changing the programme.
Monthly, not weekly. Arm circumference moves about 0.25 cm a month at best. Measuring it weekly just shows you tape error and makes a good month feel like a bad one.
